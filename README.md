## Distortion Effects _(Add grit, warmth, or bite)_
### Classic Dist
_Warm grit that never goes out of style_

The Classic Dist effect adds grit, edge, and harmonic saturation to an audio signal
by clipping and altering the shape of the audio waves.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used to emulate a vintage or hard-rock amplifier crunch, helping
electric guitars cut through a dense mix or giving a bass guitar some mid-range growl so it remains audible on smaller speakers.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on drum loops, snare drums, or drum
machine tracks (such as a punchy 808 or kick/snare combo) to add snap,
weight, and aggressive character.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used when a synthesizer or pad sounds
too "clean" or digital, flattening round waveforms to create warmth, texture,
and presence.
- **Vocals:** Best used sparingly or blended in parallel for industrial, lo-fi,
megaphone, or rock-style vocal effects rather than clean lead vocals.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Simulate an overdriven transistor circuit or guitar pedal.
  - Turn clean sub-bass waveforms into dirty, square-wave style basslines.
  - Add gritty distortion to transition risers and white noise sweeps.

#### Controls
- Dist
  - 0.0 dB to 48.0 dB
- Tone
  - 0% to 100%
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 200 Hz

### Juicy Distortion
_Thick, saturated, and biting_

The Juicy Distortion effect is used to add grit, warmth, and harmonic richness to your tracks; provides warmth and character.

#### When Best to Use
- **Bass Guitars:** Best used to give low-end instruments extra presence so they remain clearly audible even on smaller speakers like phones or laptops.
- **Electric Guitars:** Best used to dial in classic, heavy, or saturated guitar tones.
- **Vocals:** Best used to add clarity and make vocals stand out in a dense mix.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Simulate a lo-fi "broken speaker" effect.
  - Create fake sweep or riser transitions.
  - Bring out an old, distorted tape, ghostly, or antique aesthetic.

#### Controls
- Volume
  - -12.0 dB to +12.0 dB
- Dist
  - 0% to 100%
- Tone
  - 0% to 100%
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 500 Hz
- Mix
  - 0% to 100%

### Bitcrusher
_Lo-fi on purpose, a lower-res crush_

The Bitcrusher effect lowers the digital resolution and quality of an audio signal to
create a crunchy, lo-fi, or retro video game sound.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used to transform clean digital or electric guitars into retro, 8-bit
video game arpeggios or gritty, digitized textures.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on drum loops, hi-hats, or snare hits to
reduce dynamic resolution, adding crunchy, digital grit and a vintage sampler
crunch.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used on synth leads, chiptune patches, or
basslines to create a aggressive, downsampled, pixelated synth tone.
- **Vocals:** Best used on vocal chops, ad-libs, or background lines to create a
robotic, low-fidelity, or computer-filtered character.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Emulate 80s arcade game and retro console audio artifacts.
  - Create extreme sample-rate degradation transitions on risers.
  - Turn smooth pads into harsh, digital noise textures.

#### Controls
- Crush
  - 12 bit to 2 bit
- Downsampling
  - 1x to 60x

### Clipper
_Shaves peaks and adds texture_

The Clipper effect chops off the top peaks of an audio waveform when the signal
exceeds a set volume threshold.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used on high-energy electric guitars to shave off harsh transient
peaks while adding subtle, aggressive saturation.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on aggressive kick drums, snares, and
808s to control sharp peak levels and boost perceived loudness without
introducing heavy pumping.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used on aggressive EDM or synth leads to
flatten erratic peaks and keep the synth consistent in a dense mix.
- **Vocals:** Best used on dynamic, energetic vocals to control sudden volume
spikes and add edge without relying entirely on standard compression.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Create soft or hard square-wave clipping on sub-basses.
  - Achieve hyper-pop, intentionally over-driven loudness peak-flattening.
  - Tame wild, unpredictable synth modular spikes.

#### Controls
- Soft or Hard
- Mix
  - 0% to 100%
- Amount
  - 0% to 100%
- Makeup
  - 0.0 dB to 1.0 dB

### Crusher
_Roughened up by design_

The Crusher effect acts as a warm, heavy distortion and tone-shaping tool;
applies Drive and Grit, shapes Frequency Balance, and blends Wet/Dry Signals.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used on industrial or alternative rock guitars to destroy clean
tone into a heavy, clipped, and distorted wall of sound.
-**Drums and Percussion:** Best used on drum buses or acoustic snares to add
extreme mid-range crunch and raw, unpolished distortion.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used on modern bass synth patches or
leads to add intense harmonic saturation and bite.
- **Vocals:** Best used on harsh background screams, megaphone vocals, or
aggressive industrial rap vocals.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Create dirty, aggressive bass drops.
  - Transform clean white noise into a gritty build-up texture.
  - Simulate overdriven, blown-out analog gear.

#### Controls
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 800 Hz
- High Cut
  - 500 Hz to 24.0 kHz
- Gain
  - -60.0 dB to 9.5 dB
- Mix
  - 0% to 100%

### Dual Valve Saturator
_More body, texture and grit as you push_

The Dual Valve Saturator effect mimics the behavior of analog vacuum tubes to
add warmth, harmonic complexity, and controlled thickness to your audio.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used on electric and acoustic guitars to add warm, analog tube
warmth, gentle harmonics, and body without heavy fuzz.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on full drum kits or room mics to glue the
drum sound together and add vintage valve warmth.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used when digital synths sound too harsh
or thin, adding rich tube harmonics and mid-range fullness.
- **Vocals:** Best used on lead vocals to add a subtle, silky warmth, helping them
sit naturally on top of the instrumental mix.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Simulate vintage vacuum-tube recording consoles.
  - Add analog body and warmth to sterile digital samples.
  - Create soft, glowing warmth on ambient pads.

#### Controls
- Pentode
  - 0% to 100%
- Triode
  - 0% to 100%
- Tilt
  - -3.0 dB to 3.0 dB
- In
  - -20.0 dB to 12.0 dB
- Out
  - -20.0 dB to 12.0 dB
- Mix
  - 0% to 100%

### Fuzz
_Fuzzy edge, here since the beginning_

The Fuzz effect acts as an aggressive digital guitar pedal that heavily compresses
and saturates your audio.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used on lead electric guitars to create vintage 60s/70s
psychedelic, garage rock, or stoner metal sustained distortion.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used in parallel on overheads or snare drums to
add a buzzy, aggressive, retro drum crunch.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used on synth basslines or monophonic
leads to create thick, buzzing, square-wave-like distortion.
- **Vocals:** Best used on lo-fi indie, punk rock, or alternative vocals for an over￾driven, buzzy aesthetic.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Generate vintage fuzz-synth basslines from basic sine waves.
  - Create wall-of-sound industrial textures.
  - Simulate a failing amplifier speaker cone.

#### Controls
- Gain
  - 0.0 dB to 48.0 dB
- Tone
  - 0% to 100%
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 200 Hz

### Overdrive
_Raw, full-bodied light drive_

The Overdrive effect adds harmonic saturation to an audio signal, which increases
perceived loudness, warms up the tone, and makes the sound grittier or fuzzier.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used on electric guitars to emulate a pushing tube amp, adding
smooth harmonic crunch and dynamic response based on picking strength.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on kick drums, snares, or percussion loops
to add warm, punchy harmonic body without destroying the transient attack.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used on electric keys, synth plucks, and
lead lines to add subtle analog warmth, edge, and mid-range saturation.
- **Vocals:** Best used to add smooth, subtle vintage edge to rock or lo-fi vocals,
making them sound warmer and more aggressive without harsh fuzz.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Warm up lifeless digital samples with smooth analog-style saturation.
  - Create subtle overdriven edge on ambient synth pads.
  - Simulate vintage recording console input overload.

#### Controls
- Gain
  - 0.0 dB to 36.0 dB
- Tone
  - 0% to 100%
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 200 Hz
