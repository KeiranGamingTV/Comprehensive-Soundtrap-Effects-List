## Distortion Effects _(Add grit, warmth, or bite)_
### Classic Dist
_Warm grit that never goes out of style_

The Classic Dist effect adds grit, edge, and harmonic saturation to an audio signal
by clipping and altering the shape of the audio waves.

#### When Best to Use
- **Guitars:** Best used to emulate a vintage or hard-rock amplifier crunch, helping
electric guitars cut through a dense mix or giving a bass guitar some mid-range growl so it remains audible on smaller speakers.
- **Drums and Percussion:** Best used on drum loops, snare drums, or drum
machine tracks (such as a punchy 808 or kick/snare combo) to add snap,
weight, and aggressive character.
- **Synths and Electronic Leads:** Best used when a synthesizer or pad sounds
too "clean" or digital, flattening round waveforms to create warmth, texture,
and presence.
- **Vocals:** Best used sparingly or blended in parallel for industrial, lo-fi,
megaphone, or rock-style vocal effects rather than clean lead vocals.
- **Creative Sound Design:**
  - Simulate an overdriven transistor circuit or guitar pedal.
  - Turn clean sub-bass waveforms into dirty, square-wave style basslines.
  - Add gritty distortion to transition risers and white noise sweeps.

#### Controls
- Dist
  - 0.0 dB to 48.0 dB
- Tone
  - 0% to 100%
- Low Cut
  - 0.00 Hz to 200 Hz
